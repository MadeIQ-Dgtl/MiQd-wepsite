Privacy

This website

The MiQd website is a product catalogue and sales funnel hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not ask visitors to create a MiQd account on this website.

Checkout and payments

When you choose to purchase a product, you may be sent to an external checkout provider. Information you enter there, including payment and transaction information, is processed by that provider under its own privacy terms. MiQd does not collect card details through this GitHub Pages website.

Purchase delivery

The checkout provider may use information such as your email address to confirm the transaction and deliver your digital purchase. Use the provider's receipt, account or support tools for questions about information held as part of that transaction.

Links and attribution

Some outbound links contain campaign parameters so the source of a visit can be identified by the destination platform. These parameters describe the MiQd page or campaign that produced the click and are not intended to contain personal information.

External services

Pages or services reached through external links are operated by their respective providers. Their privacy practices apply once you use those services.
